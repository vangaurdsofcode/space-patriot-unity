# Authored ships in the playable fleet

The ten original fleet families and their 100 variants remain in their existing catalog slots. Kestrel K-017 is a separate prototype (`SP-K017`, index 100). Its measured 19.5 m length, 20.0 m span, and 8.6 m height do not match any existing family closely enough to rename one. The prototype uses the original family 0 pressure cabin as an interim flight deck; that interior is not approved Kestrel art.

The registered build is `kestrel-symmetric-mirror-20260926-r1`. `Validation/authored-kestrel-registration.txt` records its FBX and texture hash gate. `Validation/kestrel-playable-runtime.txt` records 14 checks in the actual FrontierGame Play scene, including the launch command, keyboard forward flight, gear retraction, collision proxies, and all three LODs. The `Validation/kestrel-playable-runtime-lod*.png` files show the prototype in the live port at each forced detail level. The original vessel from the user's save was restored before Play mode stopped.

The runtime resolver in `Assets/SpacePatriot/Scripts/AuthoredShipVisuals.cs` binds an authored exterior by exact craft designation. It requires a registered Resources prefab with three LODs, eight distinct renderers per LOD, UV0 on every mesh, populated base/normal/metallic maps, and dimensions matching the craft catalog. The resolver rotates the Blender +X nose to gameplay +Z and seats the lowest landing-foot geometry at the game's surface contact height. It attaches eight simple collision proxies to the LOD0 parts. These are temporary physical proxies; artists should replace them with reviewed convex hulls as the ship geometry is refined.

To register a later reviewed Kestrel build, first import it through `LocalShipImporter.ImportBuild(buildId)`, then run `AuthoredShipRegistration.RegisterBuild(buildId)` in the connected Unity Editor. Registration verifies the saved three-LOD UV report, all 12 source texture hashes, the imported FBX and nine Unity texture hashes, material maps, and measured size. It copies the approved prefab to a versioned `Resources/AuthoredShips/KestrelK017/` path and updates `Resources/AuthoredShipVisuals.json`. The same exact-designation registry can accept future craft without replacing every variant in a family.

Remaining work for Kestrel: repair the detached gear mounts, projection seams and wing/drive fit; author a matching cockpit and walkable interior; make rigged landing gear, damage, propulsion and cargo attachments; replace basic collision boxes with shaped proxies; establish an LOD and texture budget across the whole fleet. The other 100 craft still use their original exteriors until their own assets pass review and registration.
