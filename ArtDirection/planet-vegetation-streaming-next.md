# Full-sphere vegetation: implementation design

Historical inspection: 2026-09-26. The coordinated spherical vegetation implementation and its actual Unity acceptance evidence are now recorded in [spherical-vegetation-results.md](spherical-vegetation-results.md). The limitations below describe the inspected pre-implementation baseline; remaining visual/profile limitations are called out in the results report.

The 2,700 m cap can be removed once placement, habitat sampling and streaming keys use a planet-surface frame. Deleting the distance checks alone would reproduce northern terrain on the southern hemisphere, orient plants along the wrong up axis and clamp habitat to the edge of the port map.

The project has no class named `ContinuousPlanetSurface`. The closed planet is the **“Continuous spherical terrain”** mesh/collider built in [FrontierPlanet.cs](../Assets/SpacePatriot/Scripts/FrontierPlanet.cs), with a separate **“Worldworks / moving terrain patch”**. That file supplies the relevant APIs.

`Height(x,z)` and `RawPlanetHeight(x,z)` are northern-cap APIs. Their positive square root reconstructs the upper hemisphere and becomes degenerate at/beyond the equator. Two points with equal x/z but opposite radial Y must never share a vegetation surface sample, exclusion decision or persistent cell ID. The cap's origin is `(0,-18003,0)`, not world origin. Derive radial up from **surface point minus planet center**; keep the terrain's slope normal separate. In particular, copying the streamed patch's current `normalLocal` as a radial coordinate would confuse a sloped ground normal with a direction to the planet's center.

The HTML behavior to retain is explicit in [vegetation-stage.js](../Tools/AssetPipeline/visuals/vegetation-stage.js):10–14 and 59–73: establish a local tangent basis, project candidate offsets back onto the sphere, sample `groundHeight` and `PlanetEngines.region` using the resulting direction, and convert the result back to the local frame. Its units are kilometres before conversion to rendering metres. The source has a 5 km anchor rebase, nine near-grass tiles, a 240 m tree neighborhood, and 440/2,050 m splat build radii. Activation heights are 100 m for near grass, 500 m for full trees and 1,800 m for splats; Unity's current 16 m tree / walking-only 6 m splat gates do not reproduce those transitions. These are altitude gates, separate from each layer's camera-distance fade.

The next implementation unit should be **one shared planet vegetation sampler/frame plus migration of all three placement loops**, followed by full-sphere evidence. Its concrete scope is:

1. Add a typed sample containing planet-local root point, radial up, terrain normal, moisture, temperature, exposure, forest cover and validity. A frame contains planet-local center/right/up/forward and world conversion helpers. Give it a world-generation revision so changed planets and terrain rebuilds invalidate old jobs. Near the authored port, preserve the existing terrain/collider blend; elsewhere use direction-based sampling. Cache a small sampled ground grid per tile, following the HTML tile approach, so 12,000 blades do not each trigger collider queries.
2. Give resident cells globally stable keys such as `(worldId, cubeFace, cellU, cellV, layer)`; seed candidates from those keys, not from mutable camera anchors. Cube-face edges need one ownership rule, neighbor remapping and deduplication. This prevents north/south aliasing and regeneration jumps at rebase boundaries. Use bounded tangent frames for rendering without making those frames the identity of stored surface locations. A pole fallback must preserve a valid handed basis; retain/transport the previous tangent heading to avoid a sudden 180° rotation.
3. Migrate grass, forest and both splat layers together. Grow stems/blades along their terrain normal, offset canopy centers along local up, and include that local orientation in Gaussian instance matrices. Keep grass vertex positions in tile-local tangent coordinates so existing local x/z wind bending remains tangent. Change the Gaussian shader's world-X wind offset to an instance tangent-axis displacement; its covariance code already consumes oriented instance axes ([GaussianVegetation.shader](../Assets/SpacePatriot/Resources/Shaders/GaussianVegetation.shader):51–58).
4. Apply port/outpost/grove exclusions using actual site surface locations and local 3D/geodesic proximity, with a northern-port membership test where legacy rectangles are retained. Their present x/z rectangles would otherwise suppress vegetation at unrelated southern points. Preserve solid-world/habitat eligibility; do not populate gas giants merely because the radius cap is gone.
5. Remove every 2,700 m check only after those consumers use the shared sampler. Retain bounded resident counts, nearest-first grass tiles, distance fades and real per-frame budgets. Charge coroutine budgets for **attempted** samples, including rejected underwater/steep points. Keep the old valid neighborhood drawing until the hidden replacement is ready; cancel stale builds after a teleport/world switch and discard results whose revision no longer matches.

Source parity needs a separate explicit check while wiring habitat. The existing global atlas is useful, but [export-planet-climate.mjs](../Tools/AssetPipeline/export-planet-climate.mjs):57 samples `region(..., elevation=0)` and exports only four channels. It omits source grass height/density/width, wind shelter and plant variant from [planet-engines.js](../Reference/Original/source/planet-engines.js):45–51. Export those fields or port the actual formulas before claiming matching species/density. Also unify world profile metadata: the climate export currently assigns Venus, TRAPPIST-1 c/d and TOI-270 b a living type/humidity, whereas [Worlds.json](../Assets/SpacePatriot/Resources/Worlds.json) and [export-worldworks.mjs](../Tools/AssetPipeline/export-worldworks.mjs) classify them as desert. Finite/global variation checks do not detect that mismatch.

Acceptance evidence must include actual rooted vegetation, not merely a finite climate value or successful landing. On Earth and TRAPPIST-1 e, locate valid dry land beyond 2,700 m, at side/equatorial sectors and on the far hemisphere; verify roots against the active collider, positive outward plant orientation, no submerged roots and bounded resident counts. Walk across a cube-face seam and a frame rebase, revisit the same cells, and compare deterministic candidate IDs/positions. Test a planet transform translation/rotation, a north-to-south teleport, altitude fade bands, a world switch during a build, and mixed wet/dry/steep samples. Capture Unity views for near blades, full trees and distant splats on those sites. Existing [VegetationSeaLevelValidation.cs](../Assets/SpacePatriot/Editor/VegetationSeaLevelValidation.cs) explicitly leaves the cap in place; [DesktopPlayerValidation.cs](../Assets/SpacePatriot/Scripts/DesktopPlayerValidation.cs):105 verifies far-side landing, not vegetation. Extend their evidence rather than treating either as completion.
